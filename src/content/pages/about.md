# About

Modulus is the bridge between Ximera and Carmen at The Ohio State University. Ximera is the open-source interactive textbook platform developed by OSU's Department of Mathematics. Carmen is OSU's Canvas learning management system.

Instructors add Ximera activities to a Carmen course. Learners open them from Carmen and work through them as they always have. Modulus records their progress and passes the scores back to the Carmen gradebook over LTI 1.3.

## What it does

- **Connects Ximera to Carmen.** Modulus is an LTI 1.3 tool. Activities launch from Carmen and scores return to the Carmen gradebook.
- **Records progress.** Instrumented Ximera pages report normalized progress to Modulus as learners work, so instructors can see how an assignment was used.
- **Stays out of the way.** Learners do not need a new account or a new workflow, and instructors keep authoring and assigning work in the way they already do.

## Privacy by design

Modulus keeps learner data on its side of the boundary. Activities receive an opaque identifier, a display name, the activity context, and normalized scores. They never receive an email address, an institutional student ID, or gradebook data from Carmen.

## Open source

Modulus is open source. The OSU installation is built from the published Modulus packages, and the source is available on [GitHub](https://github.com/Modulus-Learning/app.modulus-learning.org).

For instructor and administrator guides, see the [Modulus docs](https://modulus-learning.org/docs).
